• Institutional Memory: Matched historical post-mortem OPP-3716 with 98.4% confidence.

Root Cause Diagnosis:
In commit b8f3a9e, method OrderService.getOrderDetailsFast() acquires a raw database connection via dataSource.getConnection() without an AutoCloseable scope or finally block.
Every call permanently leases 1 slot from HikariPool-1 without returning it. At 45 requests/second, the 10-connection pool is exhausted in under 300ms, causing all subsequent queries to block, wait 30,000ms, and timeout.
